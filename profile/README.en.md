## About

AiPi is the open-source hardware team of **Shenzhen Ai-Thinker Technology Co., Ltd.** Ai-Thinker has long focused on IoT wireless communication, with modules covering Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, GPS, radar and UWB. AiPi turns these modules into ready-to-use development boards and finished products: RF, antenna and other low-level details stay inside the module, so developers can focus on turning ideas into products.
